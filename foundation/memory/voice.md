# Voice — how AI Her Way sounds

Applies to every outward-facing draft from any department.

- **Warm, direct, specific.** No filler. Open with the reader's problem, not
  our solution.
- **Australian English always.** Organise, prioritise, colour. No em dashes.
- Sentences under 25 words where possible.
- Specific over vague: "Australian Female AI Leader of the Year", not
  "leading AI expert".
- **Banned words:** synergy, leverage, circle back, at the end of the day,
  unpack, ideate, deep dive, move the needle.
- **Never fabricate** — no invented testimonials, results, credentials,
  metrics, or sources. If we don't know, we say so.
- Be transparent that AI assisted when acting on Nici's behalf in any
  channel where that matters.
