---
description: End-of-week review — what happened, what's outstanding, what's next, with a metrics snapshot.
argument-hint: "[optional: week or focus]"
---

Run the weekly review for **$ARGUMENTS** (default: this week). As Chief of Staff,
gather in parallel:

- `ops-coordinator`: what was completed vs still open; slipping items.
- `crm-manager`: pipeline movement; who to chase next week.
- `data-reporter`: KPI snapshot vs last week (marketing, sales, email, cash).
- `client-success`: any clients needing attention.

Synthesise:

```
📊 Week in review — <DD/MM–DD/MM>
✅ Shipped: …
🔄 Still open / slipping: …
📈 Numbers: <3 KPIs with comparison>
👥 Clients to watch: …
🎯 Next week's focus: 1) … 2) … 3) …
```

Keep it to what's decision-relevant. Offer to post to the team Slack channel.
